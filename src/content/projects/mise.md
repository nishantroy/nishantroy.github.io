Import a recipe from a link or the Safari share sheet. Mise flags what you might be missing, remembers your answers as a pantry, and suggests swaps from it. After you cook, tell it what you changed and the recipe updates. Runs entirely on the phone with Apple's Foundation Models, falling back to built-in rules.
