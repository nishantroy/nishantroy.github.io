Import a recipe from a link or the Safari share sheet. Mise flags what you might be missing, remembers your answers as a pantry, and suggests swaps from it. After you cook, tell it what you changed and the recipe updates. The AI runs on the phone with Apple's Foundation Models, falling back to built-in rules.
